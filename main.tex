\entry
{2026}
{Acquired Silent Reading Disorder: A Single-Case Study}
{Zhejiang University}
{}
{Case: a 19-year-old who lost spontaneous inner speech after a month of ``eye--mind direct''
 speed-reading practice (forced suppression of subvocalization plus forced acceleration), and
 could no longer understand text when reading silently without external support.

 Experiments: a remote session ($\sim$2.5\,h) with interview, YBOCS/VVIQ/BAIS, rapid automatized
 naming and five jsPsych tasks (three writing systems, RSVP, homophone judgement, manual Stroop,
 rapid identification); then an in-lab picture--text matching task over one to ten characters
 under five pacing conditions (no aid; 4, 2, 1 and 0.5\,Hz).

 Account I proposed: a learned gating deficit in auditory imagery --- the generation is intact,
 but voluntary initiation is blocked, and external rhythm or movement can drive it.

 Follow-up: he later recovered at a local hospital after low-frequency TMS over the right SMA,
 consistent with what the account predicts. Wish him well!}
